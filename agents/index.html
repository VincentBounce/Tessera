Each dot has a brain of its own, 16 senses → 16 → 8 (5 used: thrust, turn, eat, split, a memory), its weights random at first and never trained. It senses its energy, the food under it and at three points ahead, its nearest neighbour (how near, which way, how akin, its energy), the crowd round it, its speed, age, memory, last meal and some noise. Each step costs it Living, Moving times its thrust and Eating times its appetite; the food in a cell is shared by those eating it. With no energy left it dies; when it wants to split and has enough, it halves its energy with a child, its brain copied with a tenth of its weights moved by up to Mutate. A dot's colour comes from its brain's weights (near brains, near colours): lineages show. When fewer than a sixteenth of the slots live, random dots are laid. The field grows back towards its richness (brighter green patches) by Regrow and spreads by Diffuse. Lineages: the founders whose children live, the largest first. The wheel zooms, a drag moves over the torus. The link keeps all the settings.
