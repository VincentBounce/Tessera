Liquid
Each particle pushed from its neighbours by the pressure of their density (Stiffness) against the rest density (Rest same between particles of one liquid, Rest other between two liquids: lower, they shun each other), and from those very near (Near: never clumped). A heavier particle moves less under the same push: it sinks. Viscosity: the share of a particle's closing speed its neighbours take away each step (0 to 1). A drag in the box: Drag carries the liquid along, Attract draws it, Repel pushes it, within the Brush. Particles: each particle shown as a dot over the liquid, Dot size its radius as a share of the particles' spacing (at 0.5 they touch). The wheel zooms round the cursor: at 64k and more the particles are a pixel or two apart, seen one by one only zoomed in. The link keeps all the settings.
