Battle: the kinds' creatures in two sides, red (0) and blue (1), dark to light by kind; Red → blue, how much more red is drawn to blue (negative: flees it), Blue → red the same for blue; a particle turns to the side most of its neighbours hold, blue once their share passes Tip (above ½ favours red). Wheel: each shade drawn to the one Chase ahead round the rainbow, pushed from the opposite. Evolve: species, each its own rules (the commonest's drawn above), shown by colour; a particle is caught by the species touching it, each pressing by its virulence (v; Contagion, how fast); every Mutate seconds a mutant species, its rules and virulence each moved by up to Change, laid on a small patch. Swirl: a push to the left of each neighbour, turning creatures (negative: right). A rule square: row the kind that feels, column the kind it feels; green draws, red pushes. Click raises it by ½, shift-click lowers it (−1 to 1). The reach is for 4,096 particles, shrunk with more: more particles, a larger world. Trails: how much of the last picture each frame keeps (0: none), the paths drawn. The wheel zooms; a drag turns the sphere or moves over the torus. The link keeps all the settings and changed rules.
