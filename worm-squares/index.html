Worms on squares

Paterson's worms, on the square grid instead of the triangle grid: at each corner, out by its left, straight on or its right, never along an edge eaten already; a situation (its free ways) the first time seen with a choice, the rule decides, the same each time after; none free, the worm dies.
Its rules (24), by how long they live
A corner of squares has 4 edges: a worm goes through it twice at most (in, out, in, out), so it shuts itself in soon: every rule dies within 16 steps, but those going straight on at a fresh corner, a straight line forever. Paterson's triangles have 6 edges a corner: 3 times through, room for its long worms.
